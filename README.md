# AKademy
AKademy is a project I've aimed to ease the burden of students. It will be a full fledged, one stop solution for all the needs for a student to get free of their academic burdens with resources including Lectures, notes in an interactive (non traditional way), Flashcards with spaced repetion (evidence based study techniques), AI question engine based on the level of the student and also an AI mentor and much more at one place.

The problem statement behind the idea of AKademy is: "To free up students of their academic burden (of old traditional system that isn't suited for the modern world), so that they can work on things that actually matter and also things they really want to work on. Things that will make a practical impact to the world and themselves."

The core idea of AKademy might be about solving the present needs, seeing the present situation, but with time the Traditioanl Education System will eventually vanish and finally the system of the world of AI and Internet will be the norm. But the distribution of students and learners who were ahead of the time will stay along with the community making for a quality distribution for the future that have potential in the real world and also the world of the future.

The early (level 0) protype is hosted at: https://akrabbanii.github.io/AKademy/
